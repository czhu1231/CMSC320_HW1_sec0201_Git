Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git allows for version control- it allows developers to track their code. It facilitates collaboration amongst developers, and ensures we have a record for our project's history.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: One field data science could be applied to is healthcare and pharmaceuticals. For example, unsupervised learning algorithms (ex: clustering) have been used for drug repurposing pipelines.
